The person behind the posts

Not just a username.

I'm Alex, a South African creator who enjoys discovering good food, exploring nature and sharing the moments along the way. I'm not here to pretend to be someone I'm not — I'm an ordinary guy trying new things, learning as I go and putting myself out there.

This page is a home for the things I enjoy and the businesses, places and people I get to discover. If a post helps someone find a new favourite spot or inspires them to get out and explore, that's a win.
